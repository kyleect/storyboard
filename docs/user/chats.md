# Chats

A chat is a conversation with a helper. Everything you say and everything it does is kept in the chat.

## Start a chat

1. Click **Chats** in the rail.
2. Click the new chat button.
3. Type your message at the bottom.
4. Press **Send**.

A new chat is called "New session". After your first message, the app gives it a better name.

## What you will see

- Your messages on one side, the helper's on the other.
- A grey "thinking" part. This is the helper's notes to itself. You can open or close it.
- Small cards when the helper uses a tool, such as saving a file.
- A moving indicator while the helper is working.

## Stop and continue

- Press **Stop** to end a run early.
- Press **Continue** to let the helper carry on from where it left off.

## Answer a form

Sometimes the helper needs answers before it can go on. It shows you a form.

1. Fill in the boxes.
2. Press **Submit** to answer.
3. Or press **Skip** if you don't want to.

## Save a file from the chat

When the helper writes a new file, you may see **Save a new file**.

1. Check the **Name**.
2. Choose where to keep it: this chat, this project, or everywhere.
3. Press **Save**, or **Don't save**.

## Attach pictures and audio

- Press **Attach** to add a picture or sound file.
- Press **Record** to record your voice.
- **Record & send** records and sends in one step.

Not every model can read pictures or sound. If yours cannot, the app tells you.

## Change your mind

Every message can branch. A branch is another version of the chat from that point. Nothing is lost.

| Button            | What it does                                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Edit**          | Change a message of yours and send it again. |
| **Branch off**    | Start a new version from here.               |
| **Fork**          | Copy the chat up to here into a new chat.    |
| **Regenerate**    | Ask the helper to answer again.              |
| ‹ 2/2 ›           | Move between versions.                       |
| **Delete branch** | Remove a version you don't want.             |

When you edit, press **Save** to send. The small arrow next to Save has more choices. Press **Cancel** to go back.

Fork asks where the copy should go.

## Helpers working together

A helper can hand a job to another helper. You will see this in the chat. See [Agents](agents.md).

## Other views of a chat

At the top of a chat you can switch between:

- **Chat**. The conversation.
- **Files**. Files this chat can use.
- **Structured Data**. Lists and records this chat can use.
- **Workspaces**. Folders connected to this chat.
- **Debug**. A plain list of everything that happened. See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Tidy up

You can move a chat to the trash. See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for how to get it back.
